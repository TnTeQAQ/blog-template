This is the **about page**, written in Markdown and rendered by the same engine as posts. Edit `content/about.md` to tell your own story.

This site is built with **React 19**, **Vite**, **anime.js** and **three.js**. Highlights:

- A clean-URL, single-root router with real history support — back/forward and scroll position just work
- A self-written Markdown → React engine with a themed component map
- Content and assets co-located: `about.md` sits next to its `about/` asset folder
- One unified interaction layer for the custom cursor, magnetic/tilt/parallax effects and page transitions

Everything is black & white on purpose.
